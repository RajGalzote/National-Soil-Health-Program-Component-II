About the data

Source: NSHP SRMU SHMS 2023–2026 Soil Resources Information Database (points, layers, NPK & pH). Each map point is one sampling site (pit, auger boring, roadcut, etc.). Horizon and lab rows were combined under their site.

    Cleaning applied

    • Coordinates come from the LatLong field, or from the Latitude/Longitude columns with stray spaces removed (e.g. "17. 83768"). Points outside the Philippines' extent are left off the map but are still counted and listed in the table.
    • Exchangeable K reported in mg/kg (ppm) was converted to cmol/kg (÷ 391). Unlabelled K values above 10 were treated as ppm.
    • Values reported as below detection (<MDL, Nil, "<x") are counted as Low. "<x" is stored as x/2, and MDL/Nil as 0.
    • Obvious entry errors were excluded from analytics (pH outside 3–10, Total N > 2 %).
    • The map, ratings and charts use the shallowest analyzed layer (topsoil). The point popup lists every depth.
    • Land use was grouped by the first crop or cover named in the record. Records holding only a numeric code (e.g. "1", "2") are shown as Coded (numeric).
